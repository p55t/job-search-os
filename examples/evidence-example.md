## Headline

Led a 1.5-year initiative that reduced product onboarding from a 5-step flow to a 2-step expedited flow, lifting completion +6.6% and driving 70% adoption.

---

## Context

The product's enrollment funnel had five separate forms collecting overlapping information. Completion stagnated at 86%, and customer research traced most drop-off to the duplicate-entry friction. Stakes: ~$X in annual onboarding revenue exposed to friction; competitor onboarding was 2–3 steps. Cross-functional surface: legal, clinical compliance, engineering, design, support.

---

## Action

Owned the program end-to-end. Built a data-prefill design that re-used existing customer records and a 2-step alternate path for the 78% of customers eligible. Negotiated the legal and compliance changes (specific consent text, audit logs). Coordinated phased rollout with engineering across three platforms. Ran an A/B against the old flow to confirm completion lift before full rollout.
