# Resume Bullet Before & After: The Anti-Slop Audit

This table demonstrates how to transform "AI-sounding" resume slop into human-authored, metric-dense evidence.

| AI Slop (Bad) | Human-Authored (Good) | Why? |
|---|---|---|
| Spearheaded the development of a suite of innovative solutions to drive engagement. | Launched 24/7 video chat trial; scaled to 100-250 weekly users. | Starts with verb; includes metric; no filler. |
| Utilized data-driven methodologies to enhance conversion, resulting in improved outcomes. | Raised enrollment completion from 86% to 92% (+6.6%). | Drops "methodology/enhance"; focuses on the specific % lift. |
| Orchestrated a comprehensive roadmap for membership growth by leveraging cross-functional collaboration. | Led 7 cross-functional initiatives; informed 2025 pricing strategy with $285M NPV analysis. | Defines "orchestrated" via projects; defines "collaborative" via stakeholders. |
| Recognized as an expert in cloud migration, successfully delivering on complex project requirements. | Delivered 35% cost reduction on cloud migration project at AWS Singapore. | Deletes self-congratulatory "expert" phrasing; uses the 35% reduction as the proof. |
| Demonstrated exceptional communication skills while presenting complex technical insights. | Presented data-backed membership pricing model to SVP leadership. | "Communication skills" are subjective; the audience (SVP) is the proof. |
| Highly skilled in leveraging advanced analytical techniques to provide insights and drive decision-making. | Modeled 8 pricing options; informed 2025 strategy for Amazon Health. | Removes filler; focuses on the specific decision-making asset created. |

---

## The "Human-Voice" Transformation Rule
- **If you can't put a number on it, make it a technical noun-verb pair.**
- **If you can't be specific about the tool or project, delete it.**
- **Never say you "can" do something.** Say what you "did."
